## What CAJAL Declines to Architect

- **The full seven-tier Irreducibly Human taxonomy.** The series convention reserves this for `irreducibly.xyz`. The book references and locates the gate within the taxonomy but does not need to render all seven tiers in detail. Doing so here would preempt the series-level diagram.
- **The Frictional neurobiology cascade (dopamine → BDNF → synapse → spine).** This is the supporting biological mechanism for the broader claim and belongs in the Frictional academic paper / Frictional project diagrams, not in the practitioner guide's themes chapter.
- **The Bastani / Kosmyna headline statistics.** 48% higher, 17 points lower, 55% connectivity reduction, 56% premium. These are bar/dot quantities that could become a small panel figure, but the chapter renders them as prose evidence inside a larger argument. Architecting a stats panel would shift the chapter's emphasis from theory to data.
- **The PwC AI-jobs-barometer cascade.** Same reason — supporting evidence, prose-rendered.
- **The Plus One vs Bridge curriculum-structure comparison.** Two delivery paths for AI+1. The contrast is real but lives more naturally in a programmatic admissions document than in the themes chapter.
- **The "Three Files" diagram and the "Five-Phase Gate" diagram.** These already belong to the `18-brutalist-claude-project` chapter and are architected in that file's CAJAL. Do not duplicate.
- **The Six Principles enumeration.** A typography list (Intent → Schema → Phase Gate → Labor Separation → Refusal Behavior → Current Knowledge/Deferred Action). The phase gate diagram from chapter 18 covers the structural piece.
