Figure 11.1 — Five-slice vs twelve-slice pie

The angle channel works between roughly 30° and 150°. Past that, the eye can't rank.

Five slices — wedges above 30°
Readable angles; ranking visible at a glance.
Twelve slices — wedges below 30°
Below the threshold of reliable angle discrimination.

Same total, different slice counts. The five-slice rule is the point at which the angle channel's accuracy degrades below useful.
