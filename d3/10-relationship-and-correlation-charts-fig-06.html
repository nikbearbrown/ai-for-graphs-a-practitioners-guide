Figure 10.6 — Parallel coordinates: axis order decides what you see

200 observations, six quantitative variables. The same data; two axis orders.

A. Alphabetical order: A · B · C · D · E · F
Polylines tangle; no clear structure.
B. Reordered: A · C · E · F · D · B
Highest-r pairs adjacent — two clusters emerge.

6! = 720 possible orderings. Each makes different pairwise relationships visible. Munzner's axis-order-dependence problem.
