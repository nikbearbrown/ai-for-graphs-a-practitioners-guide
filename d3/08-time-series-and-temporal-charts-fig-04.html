Figure 8.4 — Compressed axis vs gap-marked axis

Twenty-four months of monthly data, with March 2021 missing. Left collapses the axis; right preserves the calendar position and breaks the line.

A. Compressed axis
Feb 2021 placed adjacent to Apr 2021 — the slope is fabricated
violates the don't-skip-intervals rule
B. Gap-marked axis
March 2021 kept at calendar position; line breaks; gap shaded
honors the don't-skip-intervals rule

The Gestalt principle of continuity rewards the eye for tracing a connected line. When the line crosses a fabricated gap, the eye reads a faster trend than the data supports.
