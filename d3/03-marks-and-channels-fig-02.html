Figure 3.2 — The four mark types

Point, line, area, glyph — the geometric primitives every chart is built from. Each carries a different claim about the data.

A. Point marks
scatterplot — one mark per observation, no continuity claim
B. Line marks
line chart — connects points and implies values between them exist
C. Area marks
area chart, bar chart, choropleth — filled regions encode magnitude
D. Glyph marks
candlestick — composite mark, several attributes in one symbol

Mark choice is already an argument. Points say "samples"; lines say "continuous"; areas say "magnitude"; glyphs say "this symbol encodes more than one number — learn it."
