Figure 12.5 — Slice-and-dice vs squarified

Same twelve-node dataset; two layout algorithms. Squarified minimizes the worst aspect ratio and keeps area perception honest.

Slice-and-dice (treemapSlice)
Elongated rectangles read larger than they are.
Worst aspect ratio ≈ 6:1. Stevens dominant-dimension bias inflates the long axis.
Squarified (treemapSquarify)
Aspect ratios within ≈ 2:1.
Worst aspect ratio ≈ 1.8:1. Area reads close to the true ratio.
