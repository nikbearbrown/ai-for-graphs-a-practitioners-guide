Figure 2.1 — Vague vs specific prompt

Every decision the vague prompt omits is a decision the model makes for you.

Vague prompt
Five decisions left to the model.
"Make a chart showing AI capability across cognitive domains."
9 words / 0 specifications
Result: a chart you cannot read at a glance.
Specific prompt
Five decisions controlled by the author.
"Vertical bar chart, 8 cognitive domains, sorted descending, zero baseline, luminance redundant, values labeled."
5 sentences / 5 specifications
Result: a chart whose ranking reads in a quarter-second.

The two prompts differ by length and by the number of decisions named. Specificity is not politeness; it is the explicit transfer of authority from the model to the author.
