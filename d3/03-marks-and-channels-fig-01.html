Figure 3.1 — Position vs luminance

Same fifty countries. Same question: which countries have the highest life expectancy? Position answers it in under a second; luminance does not.

Position encoding (rank 1)
y-axis: life expectancy · x-axis: GDP per capita
Luminance encoding (rank 6)
all dots on one row · darker = higher life expectancy

Each panel shows the same fifty country dots in the same x-order. The right panel collapses y-position and re-encodes the same life-expectancy value as dot luminance.
