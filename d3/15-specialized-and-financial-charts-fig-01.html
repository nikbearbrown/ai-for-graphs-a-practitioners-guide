Figure 15.1 — Candlestick anatomy versus a closing-price line chart

The candlestick packs open, high, low, and close into one glyph using position throughout. The line chart of closing prices encodes one variable and discards three.

A. Single candlestick — annotated
Body spans open→close; wicks reach high above and low below.
B. Line chart of the same closes
Same eight periods — trend visible, daily range invisible.

The candlestick encodes four variables per period; the line chart encodes one. The efficiency is the convention's claim to exist.
