Figure 2.3 — The four-move prompt

Show. Say. Constrain. Verify. The four moves take ~90 seconds longer than a vague prompt — and reliably produce charts that are right on the first attempt.

Four-move prompt structure Four panels, one per move. Each pairs a move definition and example with a callout naming what the model decides if the move is skipped.

Move 3 is the move most people skip. It is also the one that determines whether the output is right on the first attempt.
