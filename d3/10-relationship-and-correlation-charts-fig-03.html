Figure 10.3 — Four overplotting strategies

Same ~5,000-point dataset, four strategies. Each reveals something different and hides something different.

1. Full opacity
Reveals nothing. Hides cloud shape and outliers.
2. Alpha transparency (0.15)
Reveals density and outliers. Almost always the right first move.
3. Jittered (discrete data)
Reveals count at each (x, y) when values cluster onto a grid.
4. 2D binning (hexagonal)
Reveals density as luminance. Individual points disappear.

The right strategy depends on what the reader needs to see. Specify it in the prompt; the default for large n is a black mass.
