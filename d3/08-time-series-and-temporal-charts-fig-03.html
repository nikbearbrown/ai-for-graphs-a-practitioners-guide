Figure 8.3 — Three encodings for one temperature series

Thirty daily temperatures (65–85°F). The truncated-baseline area amplifies the 70:80 ratio threefold; the zero-baseline area is honest but wasteful; the line escapes the area channel altogether.

A. Area, y-axis from 50°F
visual area encodes temp − 50, not temp
70:80 visual ratio ≈ 1.5×
verdict: distorts
B. Area, y-axis from 0°F
visual area encodes temperature
70:80 visual ratio ≈ 1.14× (true)
verdict: honest
C. Line, y-axis 60°F–90°F
channel is point position
y-range is a design choice
verdict: honest

Kelleher's worked example. Once the channel is area, the zero baseline is not an aesthetic preference — it is the requirement that keeps the visual ratio equal to the data ratio.
