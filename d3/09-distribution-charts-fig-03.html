Figure 9.3 — Three bin widths. Two of them lie.

The same bimodal income dataset binned three ways. The bimodality that survives across widths is the structure that is real.

A. Too wide — $50K bins
Two peaks merge into one broad shape.
bimodality hidden
B. Freedman–Diaconis
Both peaks visible, gap legible.
structure visible
C. Too narrow — $1K bins
Sampling variation produces spurious wiggles.
noise amplified

Apply the three-bin-width test before reading a histogram. Real structure survives. Spurious structure changes shape with each rebinning.
