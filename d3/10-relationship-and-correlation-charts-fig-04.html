Figure 10.4 — Bubble encoding: radius vs area

A value of 100 versus a value of 200. Two ways to scale the bubble.

Radius-linear (d3.scaleLinear)
Common error. Area scales as value squared.
Data ratio: 2×
Area ratio rendered: 4×
Perceived (Stevens 0.7): ≈ 2.6×
Area-linear (d3.scaleSqrt)
Correct. Bubble area is proportional to the value.
Data ratio: 2×
Area ratio rendered: 2×
Perceived (Stevens 0.7): ≈ 1.6×

Stevens' compression is a fact of human perception; radius encoding compounds it with an avoidable squared distortion. For Claude Code: name d3.scaleSqrt explicitly.
