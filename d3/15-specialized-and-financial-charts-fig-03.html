Figure 15.3 — Radar chart axis-order failure

Identical six-attribute values rendered three times with three axis orders. The polygon's shape changes meaningfully — it is partly the axis order, not the data.

A. Original order
Speed → Strength → Endurance → Agility → Technique → Recovery
B. Reordered
Strength → Recovery → Speed → Technique → Endurance → Agility
C. Reordered again
Agility → Endurance → Recovery → Speed → Technique → Strength

Same six values. Three axis orders. Three different-looking shapes. A bar chart of these numbers would look identical no matter how the categories are arranged.
