Figure 7.4 — Bar orientation by label length

Vertical columns work when labels are short. Horizontal bars are required when labels are long. Choice is structural, not stylistic.

Vertical columns — short labels (~6 chars)
Rotation tolerated; Gestalt continuity preserved
Horizontal bars — long labels (~22 chars)
Label reads left-to-right next to its bar
Decision rule. If the longest category label exceeds 10–12 characters, default to horizontal bars. If category count exceeds about 15, switch to horizontal with top-N or a treemap.
