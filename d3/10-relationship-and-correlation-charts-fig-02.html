Figure 10.2 — Same r ≈ 0.7, four very different clouds

Pearson r is a one-number summary. The shape of the scatter carries everything r cannot.

1. Linear cloud
Trend line summarises it correctly.
2. Curved cloud (quadratic)
Linear fit misrepresents the shape.
3. Fan-shaped (heteroscedastic)
Variance grows with x — the model is unstable at high x.
4. Cloud dominated by one outlier
Remove the red point and r drops to ≈ 0.3.

The same Pearson r fits all four. Always look at the cloud, not just the statistic.
