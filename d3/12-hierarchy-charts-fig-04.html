Figure 12.4 — Depth-limit failure

A five-level treemap collapses its innermost rectangles to sub-pixel slivers. A seven-level sunburst with 200 leaves drops its outermost ring below glyph-readable size.

Treemap — 5 levels, 10% share each
Level 5 occupies 0.001 of the chart area.
800×600 chart → 0.48 sq px at L5. Below pixel resolution.
Sunburst — 7 levels, 200 leaves
Outer ring punishes the deepest, most detailed nodes.
Ring width = total radius / depth. Outer arc = 2πr / 200 ≈ 12 px wide.
