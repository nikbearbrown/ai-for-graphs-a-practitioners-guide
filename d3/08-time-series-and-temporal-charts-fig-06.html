Figure 8.6 — Line chart vs spiral plot

Five years of monthly electricity consumption. The linear chart answers the trend question; the spiral plot answers the cycle question.

A. Linear time series — 60 months
point position along straight axis — highest channel accuracy
answers: "is consumption rising over five years?"
B. Spiral plot — one rotation per year
position along a curve — lower accuracy, cyclic pattern visible as one shape
answers: "is the seasonal pattern consistent across years?"
Trade-off. The spiral accepts Cleveland and McGill's lower-accuracy channel (position along a curve) in exchange for making cyclic structure perceptible as a single shape. Use it when the cycle is the question; prefer small multiples for cross-year value comparison.
