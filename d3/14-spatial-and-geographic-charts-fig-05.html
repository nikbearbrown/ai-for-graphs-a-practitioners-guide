Figure 14.5 — Mercator vs Equal Earth

Two world choropleths with identical data and identical color encoding. Only the projection changes. Mercator inflates high-latitude landmasses; Equal Earth preserves their true relative area.

Mercator projection
Conformal — angle is preserved, area inflates at high latitudes.
Equal Earth projection
Equal-area — map area matches real land area.

Africa is roughly 14× the area of Greenland. Mercator hides that ratio; Equal Earth respects it. For choropleths, equal-area is the only honest baseline.
